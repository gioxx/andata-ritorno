# Trademark Policy

This document describes how the **A/R** and **Andata e Ritorno** names, the A/R logo and related marks may be used. It complements, and does not replace, the project's source-code license.

## Status

"A/R" and "Andata e Ritorno", as names of this software and of the hosted service at ar.gxware.org, are **unregistered trademarks** claimed by the project's author through their public use in connection with this software. This document will be updated if they are ever registered.

The GNU AGPL v3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers the **source code**. It does **not** grant any rights to use the names, the logo or the brand identity.

## Why a trademark policy

The AGPL lets anyone fork, modify and run the code. That is the point of being open source. The trademark policy keeps one guarantee in place: **parents looking for A/R should land on the official app and service, not on a re-branded copy run by someone else that asks them to pay or to share their data.**

## What is allowed

You may use the names without asking in these cases:

- **Referring to the project:** articles, posts, talks, reviews, tutorials and documentation that mention A/R by name.
- **Stating compatibility:** for example "imports A/R backups", as long as the statement is accurate.
- **Running it for yourself:** self-hosting an unmodified or modified copy for your own family or for people you know, without presenting it to the public as the official service.
- **Forks that clearly identify themselves:** a fork that credits A/R in its history and documentation but is published under a clearly different name and logo.

## What is not allowed

Without prior written permission you may **not**:

- Publish or operate a modified version under the name "A/R", "Andata e Ritorno" or a confusingly similar name in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by the maintainer.
- Offer a public or paid hosted service under those names or with the A/R logo.
- Use the A/R logo or visual identity in your own product or marketing in a way that implies affiliation or endorsement.
- Register domain names, social media handles, package names or company names that use those names in a way likely to confuse people about the origin of the project.

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask: a quick conversation is better than discovering a problem later.

## Requesting permission

Write to [hello@ar.gxware.org](mailto:hello@ar.gxware.org) with a description of the intended use, its audience and scope, and mockups where relevant. Expect an answer within 14 days. Permission, when granted, is non-exclusive and may be revoked if the terms of use change materially.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes are announced in the repository.
