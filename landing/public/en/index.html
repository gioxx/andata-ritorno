How it works

  1. 01

    Photograph the item

    From the camera, the gallery or the clipboard. Add a description and, if you like, a category: bodysuits, trousers, sweatshirts, pyjamas. One tab per child or per school.

  2. 02

    Move it with a tap

    Every item is in one of two places: at school or at home. Select what you take or bring back and move it all at once. A/R remembers how long each item has been where it is.

  3. 03

    Share it with the family, if you want

    On your own, the phone is enough. With a partner or the grandparents, turn on sharing: everyone sees the same up-to-date wardrobe, and the data travels encrypted. The server cannot read items, photos or names.

Open source

All the code is public. You can even run it yourself.

A/R is free software, under the AGPL v3: the app, the server and this site are on GitHub. Anyone can read it, check what it does with data, change it and run it on their own infrastructure.

  • Free on your own, for goodWithout a family the app talks to no server: there is nothing to pay for.
  • Self-hostingA Cloudflare account and the free plans of Workers, D1 and R2 are enough. The README walks through every step, and you mint your own codes.
  • So what are you paying forA contribution to the costs of keeping the hosted service running, for those who would rather not run a server. No ads, no investors, no data sold.

Install it on your phone

Android

  1. Open app.ar.gioxx.org in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app in the A/R menu, or ⋮ → Install app.

iPhone

  1. Open app.ar.gioxx.org in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Do install it: if you only use it in a Safari tab, iOS may delete its data after a few days without use.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. Without a family everything stays on the phone; in a family the data is encrypted before it leaves, with a key only your phones know.
